About Bolo

Say what you need and Bolo compares the apps for you: rides on Uber, Rapido and Ola; groceries on Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart; food on Swiggy and Zomato; medicines on Tata 1mg, PharmEasy and Apollo 24|7.

This is a prototype. Prices and times are estimates, and no real booking or payment is made. Your profile and history stay in this browser.

Voice works best in Chrome. Try “Book me a cab to college by 12:30 pm” or “Mujhe jaldi auto chahiye”.
